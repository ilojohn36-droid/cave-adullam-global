WHO WE ARE

About Cave Adullam

This is Cave Adullam, a ministry ordained by God and sent to the Celestial Church of Christ with the mandate of revival and restoration of apostolic balance to the Church by restoring neglected true patterns of worship.

Currently, the ministry is centred around transformational discipleship in Celestial students' parishes nationwide. By God's grace, we are privileged to have planted fellowships in over 20 campuses across the nation, headed by coordinators from the ministry. These fellowship units hold both physical and virtual gatherings.

Cave Adullam also has a dedicated ministry to kids and teens, with a focus on transformational discipleship and helping young people grow in their relationship with Christ.

Through these expressions of the ministry, we seek to raise believers who are grounded in Christ, spiritually mature, and equipped to become agents of revival and restoration in the Church and society.

OUR VISION

A Revived & Transformed Generation

To see a revived and transformed generation of believers who embody the true patterns of apostolic worship, walk in spiritual maturity, and become agents of revival and restoration in the Church and society.

OUR MISSION

Raising Christ-Centred Believers

To raise and disciple a generation of Christ-centred believers through transformational discipleship, sound apostolic teaching, mentorship, fellowship, and true patterns of worship, equipping them to carry the fire of revival and advance God's kingdom across campuses, communities, and generations.

CAVE ARMY

Grow. Learn. Be Equipped.

The Cave Army is a community of believers committed to spiritual growth, transformational discipleship, mentorship, and the advancement of God's kingdom.

Cave Army membership grants access to mentorship that provides knowledge and insight expedient for spiritual growth, as well as inspired teachings from our Ministry Lead, Bro. Onipede Jesse.

It is a platform for believers who desire to grow deeper in Christ, gain spiritual understanding, receive guidance, and walk intentionally in their God-given assignment.
